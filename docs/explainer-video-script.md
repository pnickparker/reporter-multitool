# Explainer video scripts

*Draft 2026-10-14. Two short videos, each recorded on a phone screen with a voice-over. Plain language, no jargon. Update the on-screen steps if the app changes. Replace `[support email]` and the web address before recording.*

Support plan: email + written docs (`docs/user-guide.md`, `docs/faq.md`) + these two videos. Keep each under three minutes; people watch the first minute.

---

## Video 1 — "Your first interview in three minutes" (≈ 2:30)

**Goal:** a new reporter captures something, sees it process, and knows what to check.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Phone, Home screen. | "This is Mobile News Bureau. You record an interview; it gives you a transcript, the best quotes, and draft social posts. Here's the whole thing, start to finish." |
| 0:15 | Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. | "First, add it to your home screen so it opens like an app. Do this once, before you start." |
| 0:30 | Phone's Voice Memos app recording, then stop. | "For an interview, record with your phone's own voice recorder. It's the most reliable way to capture a long conversation." |
| 0:45 | Home → **Upload** → pick the recording. | "Back in the app, tap Upload and choose your recording." |
| 1:00 | Progress percentage; airplane-mode toggle on, then off. | "If your signal drops, don't worry. It waits, then carries on from where it stopped. If you ever see Try again, tap it. Never re-record." |
| 1:20 | Processing steps with the timer; app closed and reopened. | "You'll see each step. You can close the app — it keeps going." |
| 1:35 | Project page: transcript with speaker labels. | "When it's done you get the transcript. Speaker labels are a best guess, so check them." |
| 1:50 | Tap "Speaker A", type a name. | "Tap a speaker to give them a real name. It shows in the transcript and in anything you export." |
| 2:05 | Best quotes list; tap a time chip; quote plays and stops. | "Here are the best quotes. Tap the time and it plays just that quote. Always listen before you use it." |
| 2:20 | Export text. | "Export text gives you the transcript, quotes and posts to paste where you need them." |
| 2:30 | Home screen. | "That's it. Questions? Email [support email]." |

**Don't forget to say:** it's a suggestion engine — the reporter is the editor.

## Video 2 — "Trust, but check" (≈ 2:00)

**Goal:** explain what the safeguards do and don't do, so reporters use the tool responsibly.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | A quote card. | "Every quote you see has been checked against the transcript." |
| 0:15 | Highlight a quote's words appearing in the transcript. | "If the AI got a word wrong, the app swaps in the exact words that were said — or throws the quote away. It never shows words nobody said." |
| 0:35 | A quote marked "Adjusted to match the transcript word for word". | "This label means the AI had skipped some words, and the app put them back." |
| 0:50 | A post draft with "Check before posting". | "In social post drafts, if wording in quotation marks isn't in the transcript, you'll see this warning. Fix it before you post." |
| 1:10 | Text on screen: **Words said ≠ fair in context**. | "The check tells you the words were said. It can't tell you whether a quote is fair in context — and it can't catch a mistake in the transcript itself. That's your job." |
| 1:30 | Play a quote against the transcript line. | "Names and numbers are where transcripts slip. Listen before you publish." |
| 1:45 | Speaker labels wrong → **Re-run…**. | "If speaker labels look wrong, re-run and tell it how many people were talking." |
| 1:55 | Home screen. | "Questions? [support email]." |

## Recording notes

- Screen-record with the iPhone's built-in screen recording; mute notifications; use a test project with a non-sensitive recording.
- Record the voice-over separately and slowly. Short sentences.
- Add captions; many people watch without sound.
- A third short video (optional, later): "Saving a recording when the signal is bad" (Try again, Save to phone, Transcribe now).
